ATC Chair-Fly Trainer

v3.12
Purpose of this app:  Develop the habit/instinct to Listen and Set… then Read Back.
  • LISTEN: you hear your call sign… and a word: turn, or climb, or contact… so
  • SET: your right hand needs to be on the move to the correct knob in the panel, ready to dial in the instruction (tap the knob, then dial it in with the knob or on the PFD), then
  • READ BACK: what you Set
